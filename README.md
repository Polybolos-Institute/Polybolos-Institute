## Mission

Polybolos Institute is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit research organization built around a single question:

How do defense systems fail under stress, and how do we find those failure modes before an adversary does?

Named after the Polybolos, the ancient world's early repeating ballistic engine (circa 300 BC), the institute studies engagement authority when time is short, magazines are finite, and networks are contested. Not in theory. Under stress.

## Core research

- Decision latency and timing collapse under saturation
- Engagement behavior when communications fail
- Deterministic authority in denied or degraded environments
- Resource allocation under pressure
- Human-on-the-loop decision authority: preserving commander intent when automation is present

## COMMAND HOTL: Assured Decision C2

COMMAND HOTL is a deterministic, auditable Decision-C2 / human-on-the-loop authority layer. It is designed to sit above effector and C2 stacks rather than replace them. Standing Orders and pre-authorized policy keep lawful decisions moving when the link is down (tested under controlled inject scenarios; feed-to-decision median 15 ms, max 51 ms documented). Humans retain override. Actions remain auditable. Core authority logic does not require cloud connectivity.

Assessed at **TRL 5** (lab, SITL, controlled demo, Lattice developer sandbox). Proven paths include Anduril Lattice sandbox interoperability (Jul 2026 soak: 4997/5000 accepted; kin-checksum 4997/4997 ok) and Standing Orders continuity under controlled inject. Broader C2 interoperability (e.g. Link 16, IBCS, Aegis, ATAK families) remains a research and integration roadmap, not a claim of fielded multi-stack operational deployment.
