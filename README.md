# Polybolos Institute

**Assured Command. Comms Optional.**

## Mission

Polybolos Institute is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit research organization built around one question:

How do defense systems fail under stress, and how do we find those failure modes before an adversary does?

Named after the Polybolos, the ancient world's early repeating ballistic engine (circa 300 BC), the institute studies engagement authority when time is short, magazines are finite, and networks are contested. Not in theory. Under stress.

## Focus

Behavior at the edge: where decision latency collapses outcomes, where saturation overwhelms response, where AI shifts from asset to liability, and where command freezes when communications die.

## Core research domains

- Decision latency and timing collapse
- Engagement behavior under saturation
- Resource allocation under pressure
- Human-on-the-loop authority under denied or degraded communications
- Integration of decision-authority layers with existing C2 architectures

## COMMAND HOTL: Assured Decision C2

COMMAND HOTL is a deterministic, auditable Decision-C2 / human-on-the-loop authority layer. It preserves commander intent, supports Standing Orders when the link is down, and is designed to sit above platform C2 rather than replace it. It does not require cloud connectivity for core authority logic.
